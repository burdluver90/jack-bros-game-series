How to find these in the game

  1. After the poolrooms, pick Habitable zone to go to Level 1.
  2. Search the crates for almond water and the crowbar, then follow the lights into the Dark Hall and hide fast.
  3. Behind the pillar, Follow the music to Level Fun, or take the stairwell to skip it.
  4. At the party, Sneak along the wall, then ease the grey door open. Both routes rejoin the main story at "You made it."
  5. There are 4 new Level Fun endings to find: the cake, smiling back, the red door, and getting caught.
  6. Near the end, after the virus mutates, "Be quiet or they'll hear you" leads back into Level Fun with the virus on your side. That section has 3 more endings and 2 ways to escape.
